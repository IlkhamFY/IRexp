Machine-learning models for infrared (IR) spectroscopy are mostly trained on computed spectra. Simulated spectra differ from measured ones, and large, open experimental IR datasets remain scarce. Better models need real experimental data.

We therefore submit our manuscript entitled ``\emph{IRexp: A database of experimental infrared band lists from open-access literature}'' for consideration as a Data Descriptor in \emph{Scientific Data}.
IRexp holds 230{,}946 experimental IR band lists (cm$^{-1}$ peak positions) from 19{,}859 open-access articles and their supporting information, together with a Chemotion repository deposit.
In total, 96{,}973 records are linked to a resolved structure, and 50{,}682 of these also carry author-reported $^{1}$H and $^{13}$C NMR.
This is more than four times as many experimental IR records as existing public IR spectral libraries. Every record keeps its source identifier and licence.

The data are available on Hugging Face (\href{https://huggingface.co/datasets/ilkhamfy/IRexp}{\texttt{ilkhamfy/IRexp}}) and Zenodo (\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24117617}{\texttt{10.5281/\linebreak[0]zenodo.24117617}}).
The code is in the \texttt{code/} folder of \href{https://github.com/IlkhamFY/IRexp}{\texttt{github.com/\linebreak[0]IlkhamFY/IRexp}} and archived on Zenodo (\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24295113}{\texttt{10.5281/\linebreak[0]zenodo.24295113}}).
Record quality was checked by hand against the source documents and by automated consistency tests, as reported in Technical Validation.

All authors confirm that this manuscript has not been previously published and is not under consideration elsewhere. A preprint is available on ChemRxiv (\href{https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15009750}{\texttt{10.26434/\linebreak[0]chemrxiv.15009750}}). The authors have approved the submitted version and agree to \emph{Scientific Data}'s submission policies.
