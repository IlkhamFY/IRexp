We submit our manuscript entitled ``\emph{IRexp: A database of experimental infrared band lists from open literature}'' for consideration as a Data Descriptor in \emph{Scientific Data}.
IRexp is a redistributable collection of experimental infrared band lists (cm$^{-1}$ peak positions) mined from open chemistry literature.
The full construction corpus comprises $n=239{,}735$ records from PMC Open Access main text and supporting information and a Chemotion deposit; 101,624 are structure-linked, each with an evidence tier.
For Scientific Data redistribution, the dataset of record is the commercial CC-BY/CC0 pool ($n=163{,}931$), published on Hugging Face Datasets (\texttt{ilkhamfy/IRexp}), release v0.2, revision \texttt{9ee1aa9b2f462cef8cf1\allowbreak 8489e45a516a4b716193}.
Zenodo archives the commercial files of this release ($n=163{,}931$) under DOI \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23463965}{\texttt{10.5281/\linebreak[0]zenodo.23463965}}.

Technical validation includes automated transcription and recall-proxy checks, stratified automated consistency audits, full-corpus quarantine, a stratified expert human band-list audit, and a 150-record human audit of release v0.2 (structures correct for 68 of 70 formula-verified records; band lists complete and correct for 145 of 150).
IRexp stores numeric band lists with PMC accessions / Chemotion deposit DOIs and stamped licence pools---not absorbance traces---and is intended for multimodal training, retrieval, and spectroscopic workflows that need structured, attributable experimental peak lists.
