We submit our manuscript entitled ``\emph{ParetoMol: A Free Web Application for Multi-Objective Pareto Analysis of Molecular Safety and Pharmacokinetics}'' for consideration as a Research Article in \emph{Digital Discovery}.
Prioritizing compounds in drug discovery requires the simultaneous assessment of potency, selectivity, pharmacokinetics, and safety — yet freely available tools present these properties one endpoint at a time, forcing medicinal chemists to manually reconcile trade-offs. \texttt{ParetoMol} addresses this directly: it is an open-source web application that computes Pareto dominance across user-defined molecular objectives and provides 14 synchronized visualization tabs for interactive exploration of compound trade-offs. All physicochemical computations run client-side via RDKit.js WebAssembly, ensuring that no molecular data leaves the browser unless the user explicitly enables ADMET prediction.

We demonstrate three distinct analytical capabilities:
\begin{enumerate}
\itemsep-0.3em
    \item Safety-augmented Pareto analysis of 1,949 FDA-approved drugs expands the Pareto front 3.6-fold, rescuing 131 compounds---including metformin, captopril, and allopurinol---overlooked by physicochemical-only ranking.
    \item Scaffold-level analysis of 50 EGFR inhibitors reveals universal predicted AhR activation across the 4-anilinoquinazoline class (100\% above threshold vs.\ 50\% in matched controls; $p = 5.61 \times 10^{-10}$), a liability invisible to per-compound tools.
    \item Cross-class comparison of 92 compounds across six drug classes uncovers orthogonal predicted risk profiles that single-endpoint screening cannot detect.
\end{enumerate}

We believe ParetoMol will interest medicinal chemists navigating multi-parameter compound decisions, particularly those balancing efficacy against ADMET liabilities during lead optimization. The software, datasets, and validation scripts are openly available at \url{https://paretomol.com} and the main repository at \url{https://github.com/IlkhamFY/paretomol}.\\
